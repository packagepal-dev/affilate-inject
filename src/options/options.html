affilate-inject — settings

Use at your own risk. This tool injects your own affiliate ids and, on your explicit click, overrides any code already on the page (including a content creator's). It never acts silently and does not try to evade fraud detection. Earning affiliate commission on your own purchases ("self-referral") violates the terms of Amazon and most affiliate programs and can lead to commission clawback and account termination. You are responsible for how you use it.

Enter the affiliate ids you were issued by each program. They are stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and never sent anywhere except, in future versions, to the affiliate networks' own APIs.
